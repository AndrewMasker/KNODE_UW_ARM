12 Mar 2026 End of Day:
At this point I am convinced that learning even just the G term in the robot manipulator formula is too difficult
for a neural network to achieve with such a high dof system. This actually makes a lot of sense to me in the context
of our current approach to simulating robots. We don't use the lagrangian approach which spits out very nice analytical
solutions to these systems because they are very very complicated and slow to compute. Instead we use RNEA to compute
the bias terms very quickly each timestep. I think that asking the neural network to represent the extermely 
complicated bias term is too much. Instead, I think a good approach is to append a force (and maybe torque) vector to
the com of each link based on its world space states, mostly positional and angular velocity and orientation maybe.
In this way we could use knowledge about the structure of the robot to make the learning much much simpler. I think
each body should have a neural network that learns to take the local states and compute a force which is then folded
into the RNEA calculation of generalized coordinate torques.
